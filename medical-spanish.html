Why this is different

This isn't a language class, it's clinical Spanish.

Most language courses focus on everyday conversation, ordering food, asking for directions, that kind of thing. I trained as a physician in Spanish, so what I can teach you is closer to how a patient actually describes pain, or how a family talks through a diagnosis with you.

I live and work in the US now too, so I understand the gap between textbook Spanish and what you actually need in a US clinical setting.
