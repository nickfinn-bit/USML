The Structure

How Each Bootcamp Works

Our sessions use evidence-based learning techniques: pretesting, spaced retrieval, and active recall. Not because it sounds fancy, but because they actually work.

Why this structure? Research shows testing yourself before learning (pretesting) improves retention by 20-40%, even when you get the answers wrong. We built our sessions around this.

Our Approach

The Philosophy Behind USMLE300

There's already plenty of excellent write-ups for Step 2, some from people with much higher scores than ours. We're not here to reinvent content.

Here's what we actually believe: resources are overrated. Everyone's looking for some magical study guide, the "secret sauce" for a 260+. The truth is, most of you already have the knowledge base you need. What separates higher scores isn't some obscure book. It's your exam-taking skills and your overall approach.

For a 250+, you don't need more resources. You need strong conceptual frameworks for the 20-30 heavily tested concepts within each topic, and you need to train your approach to questions.

During dedicated, it's about volume. 200+ questions a day is a grind, but it builds something you can't get any other way: exam stamina. On test day, you'll see things you've never seen before. Experimental questions, random vignettes that make you feel like you're taking the wrong exam. If you've built the skill of quickly assessing a question, flagging it, and moving on, these curveballs won't sink you.

Our strength isn't knowing content cold. It's dissecting the exam itself. We've spent time comparing different question approaches across multiple blocks to see what actually works under pressure. That's what we teach: not just content review, but attacking the questions.
