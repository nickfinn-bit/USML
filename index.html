What Makes Us Different

Each Bootcamp Builds a Framework, Not Just Facts

You have probably heard "just do more questions" or "use this resource." But here is the problem: if you do not have a framework to organize knowledge, everything blurs together on exam day.

Each USMLE300 bootcamp gives you a ground-up mental skeleton for that subject. The core concepts, the decision trees, the patterns that actually repeat on the exam. Then you hang the details on that framework.

  • Not content overload. We cover the 20% of material that shows up in 80% of questions.
  • Not passive watching. You answer questions, debate reasoning, and teach back.
  • Not isolated facts. Every concept connects to a bigger picture you actually understand.
🦴 Example: Cardiology Framework
Shock States → CO/SVR patterns → Hemodynamic profiles
↳ Distributive: high CO, low SVR (early sepsis has bounding pulses)
↳ Tamponade vs constrictive: y descent blunted vs prominent
Murmur Localization → Timing → Maneuvers
↳ Left sternal border systolic = HCM; diastolic = AR or PR
↳ HCM and MVP both decrease with squatting (increased preload)
Post-MI Complications → Timeline → Presentation
↳ 3-7 days: free wall rupture (tamponade), papillary muscle rupture (acute MR)
↳ 2+ weeks: Dressler syndrome, mural thrombus formation

This is how we teach: frameworks you can reason through on exam day, even for questions you have never seen.
