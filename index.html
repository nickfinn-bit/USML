There's already plenty of great write-ups from people with higher scores than mine. I'm not here to reinvent content. What I got interested in during my dedicated period was exam strategy, optimizing the approach to questions, figuring out what actually moves the needle.

Resources are overrated. Most people already have the knowledge. What separates higher scores is exam-taking skill and a consistent approach. Volume builds stamina. On test day, you'll see stuff you've never seen. If you've trained to assess quickly, flag, and move on, those curveballs won't sink you.

My strength isn't knowing content cold. It's dissecting the exam itself. I've spent time comparing different question approaches across hundreds of questions to see what works under pressure. That's what we teach here.
