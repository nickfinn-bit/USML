Question first

What time does the Harrow Library close on Saturdays today?

The Harrow Library has occupied the same red-brick building on Chapel Street since it first opened its doors in the spring of 1962. In those early years it was something of a local institution, staying open until nine o'clock every night of the week, and for its first full decade that generous schedule never changed. The picture shifted in 1973, when a round of council budget cuts forced weekday closing back to six o'clock in the evening, though for a long time the library proudly held on to its nine o'clock closing on Saturdays as a nod to tradition. That too eventually gave way: in 2001, facing another squeeze on funding, the trustees reduced the Saturday hours as well, bringing the Saturday closing time down to five o'clock, where it has remained ever since.

The passage says 9 p.m. early on, but the last sentence changes it to 5 p.m. Catching late details like that is where the methods differ.
